How Movie Night Works

Pick a genre, invite the group, swipe cards Tinder-style, and watch what wins.

1

Create a Room

Set up your room, pick favorite genres or streaming platforms (Netflix, Prime, Hotstar), and get a 6-character room code.

2

Swipe Together

Friends join from their phones or browsers. Swipe right to like or left to pass using touch gestures or hotkeys.

3

Instant Match

The moment everyone in the session votes right on the same film, confetti fires and the movie is pinned to your group watch list.
